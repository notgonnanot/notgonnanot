01 / Learn

When do we use the Present Simple?

🔁 Habits & routines

I get up at seven every day.

🌍 Facts

The sun rises in the east.

💬 Likes & opinions

My sister likes music.

How to make sentences

FormI / you / we / theyHe / she / it
Positive (+)I play tennis.She plays tennis.
Negative (−)I don't play tennis.She doesn't play tennis.
Question (?)Do you play tennis?Does she play tennis?
Short answerYes, I do. / No, I don't.Yes, she does. / No, she doesn't.
Remember: After does or doesn't, use the base verb: Does he work? Not “Does he works?”
+ -s
work → works
read → reads
+ -es
watch → watches
go → goes
-y → -ies
study → studies
but: play → plays

Irregular form: have → has.

Special verb: be

I am, you/we/they are, he/she/it is. For questions and negatives, we don't use do/does with be: Is she tired? She isn't tired.

How often?

alwaysusuallyoftensometimesneverevery dayon Mondays

Frequency adverbs usually go before the main verb (I often read) and after be (She is always friendly). Time expressions like every day often go at the end.

Mia's daily routine

Mia lives in Bristol with her family. She gets up at seven and has breakfast with her brother. She usually walks to school, but on rainy days she takes the bus. Her classes start at nine.

After school, Mia plays the guitar or meets her friends. She doesn't watch TV on weekdays because she does her homework in the evening. At weekends, she often visits her grandparents. She loves spending time with them.
